# Privacy Policy: Bubble Shooter Arena

_Last updated: November 5, 2026_

Bubble Shooter Arena ("the app") is a game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app has no accounts and no analytics of its own, and we (the developer) do not receive or store any information about you.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads from **Google AdMob**:

- an optional video you can choose to watch for extra shots when a level runs out of shots, and
- now and then, a full-screen ad between games.

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

- your device's **advertising ID** and other device identifiers,
- your **IP address**, which can be used to estimate your general location (city or region),
- information about your **device and the app**, such as the phone model, Android version, app version, crash and performance data,
- your **interactions with ads**, such as views and taps.

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Google explains how it uses information from apps that show its ads here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

**Your choices**

- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Ad choices**.
- On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings (usually **Settings → Google → All services → Ads** or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).
- Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated PG at most).

The browser version of the game has no ads.

## Data stored on your device

The app saves your level progress, scores, settings and the player name you choose. This data stays on your device. If Android backup is enabled on your phone, the data may be included in your personal Google backup, which is managed by Google under your Google account settings.

## Local multiplayer

When you host or join a multiplayer match, the app talks directly to other phones on the same local network (Wi-Fi or hotspot). The only things shared with the other players in the match are your chosen player name and your game moves. This traffic never leaves your local network and is not stored.

## Permissions

- **Internet / network state / Wi-Fi state:** to connect phones on your local network for multiplayer, and to load ads.
- **Wi-Fi multicast:** so the host phone can be found automatically by nearby players.
- **Advertising ID:** lets Google AdMob use your device's advertising ID for ads (see "Your choices" above).
- **Vibrate:** for haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
